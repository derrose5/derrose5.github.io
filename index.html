Biography

I am currently a master student at Shanghai Normal University majoring in computational mathematics under the mentorship of Prof. Zhaoxiang Li. I was also an intern student at MATH+ML+X Lab under the mentorship of Prof. Aviles-Rivero.

My work lies in computational methods for PDEs, deep learning, and optimal transport. I am especially interested in physics-informed machine learning methods.
